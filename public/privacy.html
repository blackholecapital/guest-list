Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Scores Tampa collects, uses, protects, and stores information submitted through our guest list, reservation, QR code registration, and promotional services.

Information We Collect

  • Name
  • Mobile telephone number
  • Guest registration information
  • Party size
  • Location information used for guest list verification
  • Information voluntarily submitted through QR codes or reservation forms

How We Use Information

  • Register guests for venue admission.
  • Confirm reservations.
  • Verify guest list eligibility.
  • Provide requested SMS confirmations.
  • Prevent fraud and duplicate registrations.
  • Maintain operational records.

SMS Communications

If you provide your mobile number and expressly consent, you may receive transactional SMS messages regarding guest list confirmations, reservation updates, and venue notifications.

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Reply HELP for assistance.

Mobile Privacy

Mobile opt-in information and SMS consent are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Retention

Guest list information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for venue operations, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.
